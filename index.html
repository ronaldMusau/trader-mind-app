Where the money comes from
2 years · replayed
Average result per trade in R (1R = the amount risked). Green = made money, red = lost. The two dots show the older and newer year: a market only counts as a money-maker if both are green.
Strategy lab
Whole strategy families replayed on 5 years of every market, last 120 days sealed. Green = made money per trade. Dots = older and newer half. "Luck check" is the deflated Sharpe: near 0 means a result this good could be chance after trying this many strategies.
Real trades vs the plan
The bench: watching closely
ACTIVE = traded now. BENCH = not traded, but watched. A bench candidate must be positive in both development halves, survive 50% wider spreads and most time blocks, then gets ONE look at the sealed last 120 days: pass = Proven, fail = Retired for good. Swaps happen only after your yes.
News score · on trial
Your PC's own models read the latest headlines (Qwen 3.5 for forex, commodities, crypto and indices; FinBERT for stocks). The score is recorded on every trade and only used to decide once trades that agreed with it clearly made more money.
Model tournament
Each bar = money per trade the model would have chosen, on months it never saw. The last bar is the method in use now. A model replaces it only if it earns more and passes a final exam.
